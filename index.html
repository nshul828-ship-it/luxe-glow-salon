A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
first visit.

How do I choose the right service?

Choose “Help me choose” in the request form. Tell us your goals and we'll recommend a consultation before deciding on a service.

Do I need a patch test for colour?

Some colour services require a patch test at least 48 hours beforehand, according to the product instructions. We'll confirm the requirements when we discuss your appointment.

Is the price shown the final price?

The menu lists starting prices. Hair length, density, colour history and add-on treatments can change the total. We'll agree on your quote before we begin.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice where possible so we can offer the time to another guest. Contact us to arrange a new appointment.

Can I tell you about accessibility needs?

Absolutely. Add your requirements to the request form so we can discuss access, seating, sensory preferences and any support before your visit.
